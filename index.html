About Me

My introduction

My journey began with a curiosity for machines and how they work, which led me into the world of robotics. Along the way, I discovered a love for creativity — exploring marketing on-site, designing visuals, and telling stories through graphic design. Sketching has always been where my ideas first take shape. Today, I blend technology and creativity, building ideas that work well and feel meaningful.

What I build

I work on handmade robots, humanoid robot ideas, robotic arms, control systems, web dashboards, creative visuals, and project documentation. I build from scratch with a strong focus on clean design, accurate detailing, strong working, real prototypes, and continuous improvement through testing.
